# ROBUD Website Redesign — Reference Study

Date: 2026-11-18

## Objective

Position ROBUD as a credible wearable-robotics and movement-data technology company, with MOVEL presented as its first product. The redesign should feel like a serious robotics manufacturer while remaining honest about the current prototype and validation stage.

## Reference 1 — DIDEN Robotics

URL: https://www.didenrobotics.com/en/

Observed patterns:
- Opens with a concise, category-defining proposition: Physical AI for industrial applications.
- Uses a large, cinematic hero and real robot imagery to establish the product category immediately.
- Organizes the homepage around the robot portfolio, technology, differentiation, and a clear contact action.
- The product pages connect each robot to a real operating environment and explain what work it is designed to perform.
- The technology pages break complex engineering into named systems and individual capabilities rather than generic innovation language.
- Resources and press content create an ongoing record of engineering progress, company news, and field relevance.

ROBUD adaptation:
- Use a strong opening proposition around human movement and wearable robotics.
- Give the actual MOVEL hardware prototype the primary visual role; do not substitute competitor robot images or fabricated product renders.
- Explain MOVEL through wearable hardware, sensing/actuation, software, and intended operating environments.
- Keep the contact action visible and make pilot, product, and research inquiries easy to identify.
- Add a resource/news area later when ROBUD has publishable field tests, research notes, awards, and press items.

## Reference 2 — Rainbow Robotics

URL: https://rainbow-robotics.com/en/

Product reference: https://www.rainbow-robotics.com/en/products/

Company reference: https://rainbow-robotics.com/en/company/about/

Core technology reference: https://rainbow-robotics.com/en/company/core-technology/

Observed patterns:
- Positions the company around a coherent robotics identity and an origin in humanoid technology.
- Presents a portfolio in clear product families, with a consistent path from overview to product detail.
- Product pages explain what the platform is for, key capabilities, specifications, options, downloads, and supporting material.
- Company and technology pages distinguish company-level capabilities from individual product features.
- Uses a clear information architecture so customers, researchers, and partners can find product, technology, company, and support information.

ROBUD adaptation:
- Separate ROBUD (company) from MOVEL (product) and WID Studio (software experience).
- Make the product story easy to scan before showing detailed system architecture.
- Keep hardware, software, applications, and development status as distinct but connected sections.
- Add verified specifications, manuals, downloadable materials, and case studies only when the information is ready to publish.

## Design decisions implemented

- Reworked the hero to lead with ROBUD as the company and MOVEL as the first product.
- Replaced the understated purple hero treatment with a darker, more cinematic robotics presentation while retaining the existing ROBUD purple identity as an accent.
- Increased the scale and prominence of the actual MOVEL prototype photograph.
- Improved hierarchy, section spacing, technical labels, cards, product console, and mobile layouts.
- Made the technology architecture easier to scan and preserved the existing distinction between implemented, demo, planned, and validation-stage work.
- Kept the WID Studio simulation disclaimers and prototype/development disclosures visible. Synthetic metrics are not presented as real product measurements.
- Retained the existing Korean/English language switch, inquiry dialog, software demo link, accessibility labels, and search-engine metadata.
- Did not add unverified performance specifications, customer logos, deployments, certifications, or claims of clinical efficacy.

## Recommended next content additions (only when evidence is available)

1. Additional high-resolution MOVEL product photographs: front, side, worn-on-body, module detail, and in-use setup.
2. A short real prototype video showing the device and its operation.
3. Verified specification table with model variants and measurement definitions.
4. Real software screenshots clearly distinguished from simulation/demo screens.
5. Pilot / research case studies with permission and measurable outcomes.
6. Download center: product brief, manual, technical sheet, and company profile.
7. News and research notes with publication dates and source links.
